My main research interest is **deep learning theory**. I want to understand how neural networks learn: how optimization, data, and architecture shape learning dynamics, which representations emerge during training, and why some of these representations generalize.

I recently completed a BSc in Software Engineering at the University of Southern Denmark and will begin the MSc in Machine Learning at Tübingen in October 2026. My background is in empirical machine-learning research and research engineering. I am a **Research Collaborator** and member of SDU's [Applied and Interpretable Machine Learning Research Group](https://github.com/aiml-sdu). I previously worked as a research assistant at the SDU Data & Intelligence Lab and now initiate and lead projects with faculty and other researchers, working from problem formulation and experimental design through implementation, analysis, and writing.

## Research interests

I am particularly interested in **optimization and representation learning**. For example:

- When multiple predictive features are available, what determines which ones a neural network learns, and in what order?
- How does the optimizer influence the representations a model converges to?
- How are feature learning, implicit bias, and generalization related?
- When do models learn robust features rather than shortcuts or spurious correlations?

My previous research has been more empirical, including mechanistic interpretability and applied machine learning. I am now moving toward more theoretical work. My mathematical background is still developing, but I bring strong empirical intuition and software engineering experience, and I am interested in combining theory with carefully controlled experiments.

One thread connecting my recent work is the gap between what a model represents and what it causally uses. A feature can be linearly decodable without affecting an output, and a correct self-report can depend on the prompt environment rather than an internal mechanism bound to the intervention being described. These findings led me toward a broader question: **why does a neural network learn one representation rather than another?**

## Background

My applied work includes medical-image segmentation under severe data constraints and shallow-water detection from Sentinel-1 satellite imagery. I also collaborated with the DataVISards group at HKUST, taught Artificial Intelligence at SDU, and built [interactive supplementary course material](https://phomarkon.github.io/ai101/#/welcome).
